# Senior_Design
Sean Holmen

Project working on optimization of silane adhesion layers for parylene f enscapulated yttrium foil. 

Greater Purpose:
Yttrium patches that emit beta electrons after undergoing radioactive decay, for treatment of Basal Carcinomas (skin cancers) 1-3mm deep below the surface of the skin.

Research Professor Jeramy Zimmerman at the Colorado School of Mines Physics Department.

Student Team Member: Holiday Rhoades

We used the adhesion promotor [3-Methacryloxypropyl]trimethoxysilane or A-174 Silane for short to coat bare silicon, aluminum coated silicon wafers, and yttrium coated silicon wafers to test silane absorbance/adhesion properties.

We tested three different application methods: a quick dip of the substrate in the promotor, a long dip of the substrate in the promotor, and vapor deposition of the promotor onto the substrates.

These were compared and analyzed using an Fourier Transform Infrared Spectrometer by looking at absorbance vs wavenumber plots, and analyzing the formation and breakage of chemical bonds for the presence of added silane.
